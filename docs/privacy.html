Choir Corrector Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Choir Corrector handles information when you use the mobile application.

1. Information you provide

Choir Corrector allows you to select audio recordings from your device for choir analysis. These recordings may contain voices and other sounds included in the selected files.

The application sends the selected reference recording and rehearsal recording to the Choir Corrector analysis backend for processing.

2. Audio processing and storage

Audio files are processed by the Choir Corrector backend to identify musical analysis information such as intonation differences and detected analysis segments.

Rehearsal audio is stored temporarily while the analysis is being performed and is deleted after processing completes or fails.

A reference recording is temporarily retained by the analysis service while it is associated with the current choir analysis. When a new reference recording replaces it, the previous reference is deleted on a best-effort basis.

Analysis jobs and their results are maintained temporarily by the backend while the service is running. The current implementation does not use a persistent application database for these analysis jobs.

3. Personal information

Choir Corrector does not require you to create an account and the application does not ask you to provide your name, email address, or telephone number to perform an analysis.

The application currently uses a technical job identifier to retrieve the status and result of an analysis. This identifier is not intended to identify you personally.

4. Subscriptions and payments

Choir Corrector uses RevenueCat to manage subscription status and in-app purchase functionality. RevenueCat may process information associated with purchases, subscriptions, the application, and the device as necessary to provide its services.

Payment transactions are handled through the applicable app store or payment platform. Choir Corrector does not directly collect or store your payment card number.

For more information about how RevenueCat processes data, please review RevenueCat's privacy documentation.

5. Third-party services

Choir Corrector uses third-party infrastructure and services to operate the application, including:

  • RevenueCat for subscription management.
  • Render for hosting the Choir Corrector analysis backend.
  • The applicable app store for application distribution and purchases.

These services may process technical information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

6. Data security

Audio files are transmitted to the analysis backend over HTTPS. We take reasonable technical measures to protect information handled by the application and backend.

No method of transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Children's privacy

Choir Corrector is not specifically directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Data retention

Choir Corrector is designed to minimize retention of uploaded audio. Rehearsal audio is deleted after analysis processing. Reference audio is retained only while needed as the active reference and is replaced when another reference is uploaded.

9. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the application or its data practices change. The updated version will be published at this URL with a revised update date.
